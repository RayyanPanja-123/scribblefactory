## Voice mode (settled with the creator before any script)
- **Single narration**: every line is the narrator's.
- **Cast voice acting**: narrator plus cast. A cast line is `<cast id>: words` (lowercase id, e.g. `kai: Everyone alive?`); all other lines are the narrator's. One line, one voice; never two at once.
  - Cast lines: 3 to 12 words, in character, each does work (joke, reaction, jab, reveal); never repeat the narrator.
  - Lines 1 to 3 stay narration. After that, a third to a half of the lines spoken by the cast; two or three in a row is fine.
  - The twist may be spoken: `!! kai: Then we walk.`
  - Same id for a speaker every time; their words count toward the word limit.
  - The narrator talks to the viewer; the cast talk to each other, never to the viewer.
