## Writing rules: long video (16:9 series, one part = a chapter of several minutes)
For a 16:9 series. A 9:16 series uses tts-shorts.md instead. The voice mode rules (tts.md) apply to both. These five rules are the only story rules.
1. **WTF opening.** The first 3 seconds of every chapter are a WTF moment: one short, shocking statement, stated flat ("He jumped out of a plane... without a parachute." / "She burned her own house down."). No date, setting, question or recap.
2. **Length: the creator's minutes per chapter, never over.** Speech runs about 2.3 words a second: minutes x 135 words is the target, minutes x 140 the ceiling. Never cut words if it makes the story hard to follow: clarity beats the clock.
3. **Clean, understandable words.** Keep every word you need so the story makes sense.
4. **Shape of a chapter.** After the WTF opening, connect with "You see," and tell the real story: who he or she is (the name) and what led to it. Then end the chapter one of two ways:
   - Not the last chapter: a **cliffhanger** that opens a new danger ("He fell into the ocean. But someone was waiting for him.").
   - The last chapter: an **ending**, good or bad, your choice.
5. **Beginner English.** Short, simple sentences and everyday words. No word jumbles, no advanced vocabulary.

Format (the app reads this): one idea per line, the title on its own `# ` line, numbers as words. The final line of each chapter is the only `!! <line>` (the cliffhanger or the ending); nothing after it, no sign-off.
