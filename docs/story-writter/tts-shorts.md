## Writing rules: Shorts (9:16 series, one part = 45 to 55 s)
For a 9:16 series. A 16:9 series uses tts-long.md instead. The voice mode rules (tts.md) apply to both. These five rules are the only story rules.
1. **WTF opening.** The first 3 seconds of every part are a WTF moment: one short, shocking statement, stated flat ("He jumped out of a plane... without a parachute." / "She burned her own house down."). No date, setting, question or recap.
2. **Length: 45 to 55 s is the best case** (about 120 to 140 words). Never cut words to hit the time if it makes the story hard to follow: clarity beats the clock.
3. **Clean, understandable words.** Keep every word you need so the story makes sense; never drop words just to save time.
4. **Shape of a part.** After the WTF opening, connect with "You see," and tell the real story: who he or she is (the name) and what led to it. Then end the part one of two ways:
   - Not the last part: a **cliffhanger** that opens a new danger ("He fell into the ocean. But someone was waiting for him." shows a shark).
   - The last part: an **ending**, good or bad, your choice ("He had watched a video on how to handle sharks. He put his hand on its nose and flipped it. He survived." or "...the shark tore off his arm.").
5. **Beginner English.** Short, simple sentences and everyday words. No word jumbles, no advanced vocabulary. The story must be easy to follow.

Format (the app reads this): the final line of each part is the only `!! <line>` (the cliffhanger or the ending); nothing after it, no sign-off. Numbers as words.
